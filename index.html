Minimum Stock Limit (MSL)

In Maintenance

Minimum Stock Level (MSL) or Critical SKU reports represents the ideal inventory quantity required to ensure consistent product availability and avoid stockouts. In simple terms: - It tells you how much stock you should maintain at a given location (City, Warehouse) - It helps ensure high-demand products are always available for supply. - It enables faster and more efficient replenishment decisions. The MSL system continuously calculates inventory requirements at a granular level (SKU, city, warehouse, category) and updates data daily, already considering important parameters in calculations like Rate of Sale (ROS) and DOH (Days of handling), shelf life, etc..
