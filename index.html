Minimum Stock Limit (MSL)

Deployed & Under Active Monitoring

Minimum Stock Level (MSL) or Critical SKU reports represents the ideal inventory quantity required to ensure consistent product availability and avoid stockouts. In simple terms: - It tells you how much stock you should maintain at a given location (City, Warehouse) - It helps ensure high-demand products are always available for supply. - It enables faster and more efficient replenishment decisions. The MSL system continuously calculates inventory requirements at a granular level (SKU, city, warehouse, category) and updates data daily, considering parameters like Rate of Sale (ROS), Days of Handling (DOH), and shelf life.
